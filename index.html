3-Step Process

1

Register

+

Before you can vote, you need to ensure you are registered. Deadlines vary by state, so check early!

  • Verify your voter registration status online.
  • Have your state ID or driver's license ready.
  • Update your address if you've moved recently.
2

Research

+

Be an informed voter by researching the candidates and issues on your ballot.

  • Look up a sample ballot for your district.
  • Read about candidates' platforms and voting records.
  • Check for non-partisan voter guides.
3

Vote

+

Decide exactly how and when you will cast your ballot.

  • In-Person: Find your polling place and check the hours.
  • Mail-In: Request your absentee ballot before the deadline.
  • Early Voting: See if your state allows voting before Election Day.
