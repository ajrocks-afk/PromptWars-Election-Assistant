Institutional Guidelines

1. Verification of Eligibility

Ensure you are an Indian citizen and will be 18 years of age by the qualifying date.

2. Digital Registration

Register online via the National Voter's Service Portal (NVSP) or the Voter Helpline App.

3. Exercise of Franchise

Locate your designated polling station and carry valid identification for the polling day.
